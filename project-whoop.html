Project

💤 WHOOP Dashboard

A personal health command center built on top of the WHOOP API, adding the sports-science analytics (training load, HRV readiness, sleep debt, correlation analysis) that the WHOOP app itself doesn't show.

Flask PWA OAuth 2.0 SQLite Chart.js WHOOP API
The problem

WHOOP's own app is good at showing today's numbers, but it doesn't compute training load ratios, correlate sleep debt against next-day recovery, or explain the reasoning in plain language. Those questions matter more than any single day's score.

The approach

A Flask PWA that authenticates once via WHOOP's OAuth flow, then pulls recovery, strain, sleep, and workout data into a single view. Layers in metrics WHOOP doesn't expose: 7-day vs 28-day training load (ACWR), HRV readiness against a personal baseline, training monotony, and Pearson correlations between sleep debt and recovery.

The result

A "Mission Control" home view with a daily briefing and prioritised actions, dedicated Recovery/Sleep/Strain/Performance pages with full history charts, and a Performance Intelligence page that cites the actual sports science (Plews 2013, Gabbett 2016, Banister 1975) behind every formula. Installable to a phone home screen like a native app.

Screenshots

All screenshots below are of the live dashboard rendering my own 167-day WHOOP history, not sample data.

Mission Control

Recovery, full history
HRV plotted against a personal baseline band rather than a generic range, alongside resting heart rate, SpO2, skin temperature, and a training readiness score exclusive to this dashboard.

Strain and training load

Performance Intelligence
Every formula on this page cites its source: rMSSD-based HRV readiness (Plews et al. 2013), the ACWR sweet spot (Gabbett 2016), and the Banister impulse-response training load model, with Pearson correlations run on my own data.
Installed as a PWA
Responsive down to phone width with bottom tab navigation, installable to a home screen so it opens like a native app rather than a browser tab.
